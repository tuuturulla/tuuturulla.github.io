    Sara Pyky

    I'm studying Information and Communication Technology (BEng) with a specialisation in data and AI, and I'm looking for an internship in the Helsinki region for summer or autumn 2027. This portfolio collects my experiments with data, machine learning and AI: what models do well, and where they go wrong.

    About me

    I'm especially interested in machine learning, practical applications of AI, and the fuel that runs underneath them: data. I also care about the ethical and security questions that come with AI.

    I learn best by doing. Through my projects I've tried out a range of machine learning methods and worked on data preparation and feature engineering.

    Before my studies I spent two years working with people with intellectual disabilities across several units, part of that time as a team leader. I ran team meetings and kept the yearly schedule of recurring tasks on track, administered medication, and worked closely with other professionals. From that work I bring precision, responsibility and the ability to work with very different people – all surprisingly close to what data work demands.

    I'm curious as a cat, I enjoy brainstorming, and I'm persistent – but not blinkered. I look at things critically and change direction when something doesn't work. If I don't know something, I find out. If I see an opportunity, I say it out loud, even if the idea still needs a round or two. My mission is to learn something every day that I didn't know yesterday – ambitious in a field that seems to change every Tuesday.

    JunaJumis – stations where the timetable goes for a coffee

    I built a data pipeline that fetches train data from the Fintraffic API, models it with dbt into DuckDB and shows on a dashboard which stations trains arrive late to the most. The whole chain updates with a single command.
